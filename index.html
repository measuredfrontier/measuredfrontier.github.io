Measured Frontier

Explainers about space, frontier technology and AI, by the numbers.

Measured Frontier is a YouTube channel (@measuredfrontier). Every video answers one question with one dataset. Every number on screen is tied to a quoted source, speculation is labelled, and corrections are public. The narration is a synthetic voice.

About the yt-pipeline app

"yt-pipeline" is a private tool used only by the channel's owner. Through Google's OAuth, it uploads the channel's own videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, moderates comments on the channel's own videos, and reads the channel's own analytics. It does not offer any service to other people, and it does not access anyone else's account.
