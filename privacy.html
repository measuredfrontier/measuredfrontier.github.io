Privacy policy

Applies to the "yt-pipeline" app used by the Measured Frontier YouTube channel. Last updated 2026-10-30.

Who uses the app

Only the channel's owner, who signs in with their own Google account. The app has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Through the YouTube Data API: the owner's own channel, its videos, their metadata, thumbnails and captions, and the comments on the channel's own videos (to hold or remove spam).
  • Through the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs: the channel's own aggregate statistics (views, watch time, impressions, traffic sources, subscribers gained). These are aggregate numbers and do not identify individual viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos, moderate spam, and measure how the videos perform. It is stored privately by the owner and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. Public comments are read only to moderate the channel's own videos.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
